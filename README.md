# Silvia, Our Gator Girl

A gentle, looping memorial slideshow for Silvia. Each slide stays up for 10 seconds, then fades into the next, and it starts over after the last one.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)

1. Sign in at github.com and click **New repository**. Name it something like `silvia`, set it to **Public**, and click **Create repository**.
2. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in everything from this folder (`index.html`, `README.md`, `.nojekyll` and the whole `images` folder), then click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Build and deployment," set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, pick the **main** branch and the **/(root)** folder, and click **Save**.
4. Wait a minute or two, then refresh that page. Your link will appear at the top, in the form `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/silvia/`.

## Watching it

- It starts playing as soon as the page opens.
- Click anywhere (or press **F**) for full screen.
- Press **Space** to pause, and **←** / **→** to move between slides.
- It works on phones and tablets too; turn the phone sideways for the biggest view.

## Changing the timing

Open `index.html`, find `var SECONDS=10;` and change the number.
